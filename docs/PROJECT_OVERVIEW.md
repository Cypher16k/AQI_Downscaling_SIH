# Project Overview

## Title

AI-Based High Resolution NO₂ Downscaling for Hyderabad


## Problem Statement

Air quality monitoring stations provide accurate pollution measurements but are spatially limited.

Satellite observations provide large-area coverage but require refinement to generate localized pollution estimates.

The objective is to develop an AI-based system capable of generating high-resolution NO₂ concentration maps at 1 km × 1 km resolution.


## Proposed Solution

The system combines:

- Satellite observations
- Meteorological conditions
- Urban characteristics
- Terrain information
- Ground monitoring observations

to estimate spatial NO₂ distribution.


## Study Area

Hyderabad, India

Spatial resolution:

1 km × 1 km

Number of grid cells:

1543


## Key Innovation

The project evolves from satellite-based prediction to ground-calibrated prediction by integrating CPCB/TSPCB monitoring station observations.
